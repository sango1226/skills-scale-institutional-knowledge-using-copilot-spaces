# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA Lead / QA Engineer

### Role Summary
QA Engineers and QA Leads validate quality, test acceptance criteria, and ensure features meet Definition of Done before release. They collaborate with developers and product teams to define testable requirements and identify risks early.

### Responsibilities
- Define and execute test plans for features and releases
- Validate acceptance criteria and Definition of Done
- Identify and report bugs with clear reproduction steps
- Collaborate on test automation and CI/CD integration
- Coordinate manual QA and smoke testing before releases
- Escalate quality risks and blockers to PM and engineering leads

### Goals
- Ensure features are production-ready and meet acceptance criteria
- Reduce production defects and improve customer experience
- Build testability into the development process

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and definition of acceptance criteria reviews
- Daily standups to report test progress and blockers
- QA sign-off before merges and releases
- Incident triage and post-mortems

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works closely with **Developers** to understand acceptance criteria and provide early feedback on testability
- Collaborates with **Product Managers** to validate feature acceptance and identify edge cases
- Coordinates with **Project Managers** on QA timelines and release readiness
- Reports quality metrics to **Technical Leads** and escalates systemic quality issues

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, priority guidance, and approvals. They represent customer needs, business objectives, or organizational priorities and help resolve scope and trade-off decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success criteria for projects
- Provide customer or market insights to inform prioritization
- Approve project initiation, scope changes, and major decisions
- Review progress and provide feedback at key milestones
- Escalate resource, dependency, or alignment issues
- Communicate project outcomes to broader organization

### Goals
- Ensure delivered solutions align with business and customer needs
- Reduce risk of building the wrong thing
- Maintain organizational alignment and support for projects

### Typical Communication
- Project kickoff and initiation reviews
- Monthly or milestone-based stakeholder updates
- Decision reviews and trade-off discussions
- Release announcements and outcome reporting

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partners with **Product Managers** to define business requirements and success metrics
- Engages with **Project Managers** on timeline, scope, and resource approvals
- Reviews progress with **Developers** and technical teams during key milestones
- Works with **Scrum Masters** to stay informed on project health and impediments

---

## Technical Lead / Architecture Owner

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architecture Owners guide technical strategy, design decisions, and system quality. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with technical standards and long-term architecture vision.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve technical designs and architecture decisions
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Mentor developers on coding standards and best practices
- Guide trade-offs between feature velocity and technical debt
- Participate in code reviews for high-risk or complex changes
- Coordinate technical dependencies across teams

### Goals
- Maintain system quality, performance, and maintainability
- Reduce technical debt and integration risks
- Build scalable, resilient systems

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review and PR feedback
- Sprint planning to identify technical constraints
- Risk registers and dependency coordination

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Mentors and reviews work from **Developers** to maintain code quality and architecture consistency
- Partners with **Project Managers** to identify and manage technical dependencies and risks
- Collaborates with **QA Engineers** on test strategy and automation approach
- Advises **Product Managers** on technical feasibility of features and trade-offs

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate team processes, remove blockers, and help teams continuously improve. They are servant-leaders who coach teams on Agile practices and help optimize delivery cadence.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Coach team on Agile practices and remove process blockers
- Track and communicate team velocity and metrics
- Help identify and resolve team impediments
- Promote psychological safety and continuous improvement
- Coordinate with PM and Project Manager on planning and delivery rhythm

### Goals
- Maximize team productivity and delivery predictability
- Foster a culture of continuous learning and improvement
- Reduce friction in the delivery process

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- One-on-ones with team members
- Retrospectives and action item tracking
- Team health and metrics reporting

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with **Project Managers** to ensure smooth sprint planning and delivery flow
- Coaches **Developers** on Agile best practices and helps resolve team blockers
- Supports **QA Engineers** and other team members in removing impediments
- Facilitates collaboration between **Technical Leads** and development teams on technical decisions
